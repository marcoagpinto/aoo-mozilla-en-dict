# U.S. English Dictionaries for Mozilla

The U.S. English dictionaries for Mozilla products are based on Marco A.G.Pinto's dictionaries and are packaged and published as Mozilla add-ons by Jörg K.

Firefox:
https://addons.mozilla.org/firefox/addon/us-english-dictionary/

Thunderbird:
https://addons.thunderbird.net/thunderbird/addon/us-english-dictionary/
